Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I connect my wallet?

Metamask is recommended when using the site. You must use Metamask mobile or another supported wallet to access the site on mobile. Trust wallet is also recommended as it's easier for some users

If you are stuck on the "Connect" button, try disabling other crypto wallet extensions. They can interfere with the connection to the site.

What is the price of Hens?

Hens do not have a set price. Higher TVL more Hens per USDT BEP20 | Lower TVL less Hens This creates a level playing field so no user can be late There is NO calculator available to work this out.

What will my daily percentage be?

Daily percentage is up to 10%. It depends on your compounding habits, the time of day you compound, and the TVL.

The average daily return is 4-10% if you compound 6 days and claim 1 day.

Why are my rewards not growing?

Claiming too frequently will decrease your daily percentage.

We recommend a 6 to 1 compounding schedule (compound 6 days, claim 1 day) to maximize your returns.

Note: They depend on your current percentage.

What happens if I always claim?

If you always claim your daily percentage will drop dramatically and will not receive the highest daily returns.

This is the anti-whale mechanism that helps so the TVL is not drained.

Is compounding more often better, 2 or 3 times a day?

Yes. You will need to pay standard BSC gas fees for every compound.

What are "Hens"?

Hens are the miners that are hired with USDT-BEP20 and work to get your rewards.

This is just a term we decided to use.

Can I get my initial back?

Hen Hatch miner is a locked rewards pool, so no. You get your initial back over time, ususally from 10 to 28 days, through the my rewards section of the dapp.

What are the fees?

There is 6% dev fee taken upon ALL buys and 8% on sells from Hen Hatch Miner.

Standard BSC network gas fees are required for EVERY transaction made deposits withdraws and compound!

* Common issue when compounding is not enough BNB for the gas fee.

Do Hens amount decrease when you claim?

No, the 'Hens' amount will not change. If you make a new deposit or compound the amount will increase but not when you claim (take rewards).

The 'Hens' are less efficient over time if you claim too much..

Is this sustainable?

Yes, as long as there is TVL in the contract then there will always be rewards.

The people that claim to much get punished and get lower and lower rewards. It will last a long time the higher the TVL is.

What prevents the devs from rugging?

The contract is immutable. Therefore cannot be changed or altered in any way shape or form.

The contract has been verified and can be viewed at BSC Scan.

Is it too late to invest?

No, the contract is written to provide an optimal entry for any and all users, new or old.

This is the reason the Hens per USDT amount changes frequently with TVL fluctuation to provide an even playing field for any new user coming to Hen Hatch and not giving the first user a head start.

Can contract functions be added?

No, the contract is immutable and cannot be changed. The contract works perfectly well as it is.

No wallets can be blacklisted, no forcing people to compound days and claim 1 day a week. Nothing can be changed.

What is the minimum deposit?

The minimum new deposit into the miner is 10 USDT (BEP20)

If there are rewards in the "My Rewards" section and you make a new deposit both the new deposit and rewards are transferred into Hens.

What is minimum compound and minimum withdrawal?

The minimum compound is 1 Egg. You will always be charged standard BSC BEP20 gas fees for every interaction with the contract.

There is no minimum withdrawal but note that gas fees may apply.

Does it have am anti whale mechanism?

The contract has an in-built anti-whale mechanism.

This works as follows; User 1 compounds 6:1 gets highest daily percentage in that 24 hour period. User 2 does 5:2 gets a lower percentage. User 3 does 4:3 and gets an even lower percentage again.

This will continue dropping until the user's rewards drop to 0.5% per 24 hour period. This is to prevent 1 user draining the TVL, creating sustainability
